# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-01-07

## Overview
This privacy policy describes how the Snap to Clipboard browser extension ("the Extension")
handles user data.

## Data Collection
The Extension does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

## Data Usage
The Extension operates locally in your browser to capture page content and copy it to your
clipboard. No data is sent to any external servers.

## Data Sharing
The Extension does not sell, share, or disclose any user data to third parties.

## Permissions
The Extension requests only the permissions necessary to perform its core functionality,
such as accessing the active tab or page content when you trigger the extension.
